Thermostat

This is a very small language model. It has read 72 short sentences and it guesses the next word from the one before. The dial is temperature: low and it always says the likeliest thing, high and it will say almost anything.

What the bars are

For the last word in the sentence, the model counts which words followed it in its reading. Those counts become odds. A real model does the same job with a neural network and the whole conversation in view, where this one only looks back one word.

What temperature does

Each count is raised to the power of one over the temperature before the odds are worked out. Below 1, the favourite pulls further ahead. Above 1, the gaps close and unlikely words get a real chance. Drag the dial and the bars change before you pick anything.

Why it matters

Low temperature suits work where you want the same answer every time, like pulling a date out of an email. Higher settings suit brainstorming. Past a point, variety turns into nonsense, and you can see that happen here around 2.
