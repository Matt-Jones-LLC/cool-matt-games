A toy model, not ornithology: the bird steers with a simple correct-toward-the-spot rule.

The name

Windhover

An old English name for the kestrel, and the title of a Gerard Manley Hopkins poem about watching one ride the air.

The trick

Zero ground speed

The bird is flying the whole time. It only looks still because the air is moving just as fast the other way.

The lesson

Small corrections

Holding a spot isn't one big effort. It's a stream of tiny adjustments, made before the drift gets large.
