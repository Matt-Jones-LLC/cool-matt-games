How good is the agent's reply?

Keys: 1 to 4 to grade, Enter for the next reply. Every customer, company and ticket here is made up, and the transcripts were written with Claude for this game.

One grade, four choices

Real quality rubrics score five or six categories per ticket. They mostly collapse to this: a model answer, good enough to send, needs work, or should never have been sent. One call per reply keeps the game quick.

You grade first

The reviewer's grade stays hidden until you commit. If you saw it first you would drift towards it. The same order works when an AI model does the first pass of quality review: the person decides, then compares.

Friendly isn't the same as right

A few replies are warm, apologetic and badly wrong: a refund outside policy, a password used, personal data read out. Tone hides those. They are the ones worth catching.
