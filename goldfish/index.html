Goldfish

You are a language model with a very short memory. Words arrive one at a time, and the oldest ones fall out as new ones come in. Then you get one question: is this word still in your context?

Goldfish Context: 3 wordsScore: 0Best: 0

Your context holds the last 3 words you saw. Everything older is gone.

Keys: Y or ← for still in context, N or → for forgotten, Space to continue. Your best score is kept in this browser only.

What a context window is

A language model reads a fixed amount of text at once. In a long conversation, the oldest text has to be dropped or summarised to make room. The model isn't being careless when it loses track of something from an hour ago. That text is no longer in front of it.

Why it gets harder

Each right answer in a row adds to your score, and every third one makes the window a word longer and the stream a little faster. A bigger window means more to hold, and the word that just fell off the end is the one you're most likely to get wrong.

What the game leaves out

Real windows are measured in tokens, not words, and hold many thousands of them. Real models also don't answer yes or no about their own context. The sliding behaviour is the real part.
