Slick Shift

Someone waxed the warehouse floor and you're the robot on the night shift. Once you start moving you don't stop until you hit something. Collect every battery, then get to the charger. Twelve levels.

Slick Shift

Keys: arrows or WASD to move, Z to undo, R to restart, H for a hint. On a phone, swipe on the board or use the arrow pad. Progress is saved in this browser.

Every level can be solved

The levels were picked by a program that plays each candidate floor plan to the end and keeps the ones with the longest shortest solution. The “best possible” number is that shortest solution, so three stars means you matched it.

The hint is worked out live

Hint doesn't read from an answer sheet. It searches every sequence of moves from where you are now and shows the first step of the shortest one. The same search is how the game knows when you've trapped yourself.

Seven things on the floor

Batteries, pits, sticky mats, cracked tiles that break after one crossing, a button that swaps which doors are open, conveyors that turn you, and teleporter pairs. Each is introduced on its own before the later levels combine them.
