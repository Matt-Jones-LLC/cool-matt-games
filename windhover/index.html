Windhover

A kestrel hovers over a field and spots what doesn't belong. Windhover does that for geofences. There are six fences on this map and four checks on each. The dotted outlines are the true property lines. Click a fence and drag its handles until the report comes back clean.

Swipe the map sideways to see it all · drag the round handles to reshape a fence

0 / 6Fences passing

    A sandbox with made-up sites. Areas are measured by sampling the map on a grid, so percentages are approximate.

    Check 1 · Shape

    No bow-ties

    A polygon whose edges cross itself has no reliable inside. Usually two corners were clicked in the wrong order.

    Checks 2 & 3 · Fit

    Cover it, don't spill

    A fence should cover at least 95% of the property and keep spill past the line under 15%. A circle around a rectangle can't do both.

    Check 4 · Neighbors

    One truck, one site

    Where two fences overlap, a truck is "at" both customers at once. Dwell time gets counted twice and nobody trusts either number.
