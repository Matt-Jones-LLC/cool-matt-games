The games and the moving pictures are made here. The 19 recorded sounds were shared by generous people on Freesound, and found through Openverse.

How the sounds are licensed

15 of the sounds are CC0, which means their makers gave them to the public domain. 4 are CC BY 4.0, which asks for credit, so here it is. All of them have been changed a little: the games play them faster, slower, louder or quieter than the originals, and trim silence from the start of some.

If a sound here is yours and you'd like it credited differently or removed, get in touch.
