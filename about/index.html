Who's Matt?

I'm Matt Jones, an AI designer in Texas, and Cool Matt Games is where I put what I make. I work out what a small interactive thing should do, then build it with Claude until it works on a phone and makes sense to someone seeing it for the first time.

The site is meant to be somewhere you'd visit on purpose, the way the game sites of the Flash years were. There are games, tools, toys and widgets on the home page, and each one runs right there in the browser with no sign-up and nothing to install.

Some of it comes from customer support work: grading replies, and handing tickets across time zones. Some comes from an interest in maps and geofences that fire when they shouldn't. Some of it is there to make an idea about language models easier to see, like what a context window is or what temperature does. A few things are only there because they were fun to make.

If you want something like this for your own product or team, email me or book a time.
